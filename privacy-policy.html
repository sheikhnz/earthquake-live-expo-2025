Privacy Policy

Earthquake Live

Effective: March 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Earthquake Live ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free, ad-supported mobile application developed by AverageDevs that displays real-time earthquake data from the USGS Earthquake Hazards Program. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

2. Information We Collect

Location Data (optional)

If you grant permission, the app accesses your device's approximate or precise location solely to prioritize earthquakes near your area on the map. Location data is processed on-device and is never transmitted to our servers or stored remotely.

Advertising Data

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising identifier (Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • IP address (for approximate location-based ad targeting)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)

This data is collected by Google and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have direct access to this data.

No Personal Information

The app does not directly collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • USGS Earthquake Catalog API — to fetch real-time earthquake data. No user data is sent to the USGS. API docs.
  • Google Maps SDK — to render the map view. Google may collect anonymized usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob — to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data as described in Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Expo / EAS — used to build and distribute the app. Expo may collect crash and diagnostic data as described in Expo's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising & Data Sharing

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver relevant ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers and usage data. We do not sell or trade your personal data directly. However, ad-related data is shared with Google's advertising network as part of the AdMob service.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings or by resetting your device's Advertising ID in your device settings.

5. Data Retention

The app does not store any user data on external servers. All location data is processed locally on your device and is discarded when the app is closed.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke location permission at any time through your device settings.
  • The app will continue to function without location access — earthquakes will simply not be sorted by proximity.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads via your device's Ad Settings or by resetting your Advertising ID.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
